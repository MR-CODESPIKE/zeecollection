The Atelier Heritage

The Soul of Zee Collection

Founded in Victoria Island, Lagos, Zee Collection was born out of a desire for modest couture that reflects both timeless West African royal dignity and contemporary Middle Eastern craftsmanship.

Every garment begins with natural textiles: heavy Mulberry silks that glide over the body without clinging, structured Japanese Nidhas with rich hand-feel, and double georgette crepes that flow with every step.

Master Embroidery

Every bullion thread, crystal embellishment, and braided sfifa is applied by hand in limited batches.

Generous Proportions

No scrimping on length or width. Full sweeping silhouettes designed for confidence and modest grace.

Bespoke Consultations

Custom length adjustments and bridal party orders arranged directly with our atelier stylists.

Velvet Box Packaging

Every delivery arrives boxed in our signature gold-embossed ivory and charcoal velvet packaging.
